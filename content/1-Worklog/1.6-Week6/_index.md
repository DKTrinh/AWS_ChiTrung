### Weekly Topic

System measurement and alerting (Amazon CloudWatch) + Infrastructure management via code (AWS CloudFormation)

### Weekly Objectives

* Deploy monitoring and automated log collection systems for AWS resources.
* Automate the resource provisioning process by writing CloudFormation templates.
